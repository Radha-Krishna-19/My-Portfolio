Silent Voice

Indian Sign Language ↔ English translator, built as a team of 2.

What it does

A webcam feed is turned into hand and body landmarks with MediaPipe; a 1D-CNN trained on the INCLUDE dataset (chosen over a BiLSTM after benchmarking both) classifies the signs, and recognised signs are turned into English sentences. It also works the other way, from English to sign glosses.

What I built

  • The sign-recognition models: BiLSTM vs 1D-CNN benchmark
  • The full-stack app: FastAPI backend and React frontend
  • Live streaming between them over WebSockets
  • The step that turns recognised signs into sentences
